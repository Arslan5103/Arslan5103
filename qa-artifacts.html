BUG-DIQ-418

State reset in Deal Paperwork fees table upon navigating to Carrier Shipment tab

Project: DealerIQ AI • Environment: Staging (v2.4.1) • Reporter: Muhammad Arslan

Severity: High Priority: P1 Status: Verified & Closed
Steps to Reproduce
  1. Open active Deal #9482 in pipeline.
  2. Verify active paperwork shows itemized accessory fee ($850.00) and Total = $42,850.00.
  3. Click "Carrier Shipment" tab to inspect dispatch details.
  4. Click back to "Paperwork & Fees" tab.
  5. Observe accessories table and total calculation field.
Expected Result

Paperwork state and accessories persist across view changes within the same session.

Actual Result

Tab routing unmounted component without saving cache, clearing accessories and resetting total to $42,000.00.

Business Impact: Dealership sales managers would quote inaccurate discounted contracts during in-person negotiations. Fixed with state persistence hook and verified on Chrome, Edge, Safari.
BUG-LDL-209

Backend email worker crashed on CSV leads containing unescaped apostrophes

Project: Leadly.ai • Environment: Staging Worker / Node.js • Reporter: Muhammad Arslan

Severity: Critical Priority: P1 Status: Verified & Closed
Steps to Reproduce
  1. Upload CSV with contact names containing apostrophes (e.g., "Patrick O'Connor").
  2. Trigger multi-step email sequence using variable {{first_name}}.
  3. Monitor BullMQ worker process logs.
Expected Result

String sanitized; emails dispatched cleanly to all 500 contacts.

Actual Result

Apostrophe broke raw SQL query concatenation, crashing queue worker process and dropping 142 campaign emails.

Business Impact: High prospect data loss and stalled sales pipelines. Prevented by introducing parameterized template compiler.
BUG-BFR-104

Embeddable booking modal fails to expand height on iOS Safari iframe container

Project: Befer AI • Environment: Mobile Safari (iOS 17) • Reporter: Muhammad Arslan

Severity: High Priority: P2 Status: Verified & Closed
Steps to Reproduce
  1. Open embedded client booking form on iPhone Safari.
  2. Select service category and pick an appointment slot.
  3. Observe confirmation screen layout.
Expected Result

Iframe expands to full content height via window postMessage communication.

Actual Result

Container clipped at 320px, hiding Confirm CTA below viewport fold with no scrollbar.

Business Impact: 100% bounce rate on mobile iOS users attempting to complete contractor bookings.
